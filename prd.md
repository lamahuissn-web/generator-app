Product Requirement Document (PRD)1. Executive SummaryA web-based platform tailored for private electricity generator operators in Lebanon to streamline subscriber management, meter reading, cash collections, operational expense tracking, and customer communication via WhatsApp. The system supports both computer and mobile interfaces for desktop admins and field collectors, with native dual-language support (English and Arabic).2. User Roles & AccessAdmin / System Owner: Full access to metrics, financial reports, expense entries, subscription controls, audit logs, and WhatsApp broadcasts.Field Collector: Mobile-first interface to view assigned/searchable routes, enter meter readings, record cash collections, view subscriber balance histories, and send individual receipts via WhatsApp.3. Subscriber & Subscription ManagementA. Subscription TypesMetered Subscribers (meter): Billed using monthly usage readings plus a fixed maintenance fee based on amperage.$$\text{Total Bill} = [(\text{Current Reading} - \text{Previous Reading}) \times \text{kWh Price}] + \text{Fixed Maintenance Fee} + \text{Previous Unpaid Debt}$$Zero Usage Logic: If usage is zero, the system generates a bill covering only the maintenance fee and outstanding balance.Flat-Rate Subscribers (flat_rate): Pays a fixed monthly fee based on allocated amperage, completely bypassing meter reading inputs.$$\text{Total Bill} = \text{Fixed Monthly Flat Amount} + \text{Previous Unpaid Debt}$$B. Route & Subscriber AssignmentDefault Routes: Subscribers are grouped geographically by neighborhoods/routes for daily field visits.Global Search Access: Collectors can search for any subscriber system-wide (by name, meter ID, or phone) to accept payments outside their assigned route.4. Field Operations & Collection LogicA. Collection WorkflowCollector selects a route or searches for a subscriber.For metered accounts, collector enters the Current Meter Reading. The app computes consumption and the total balance.Collector inputs the Cash Amount Received (supports full or partial payment).System immediately credits the payment, updates the outstanding debt, and generates a WhatsApp receipt option.B. Partial Payments & RolloversPayments reduce the total outstanding balance ($\text{Previous Debt} + \text{Current Bill}$).Unpaid balances roll over automatically to the following month as Outstanding Balance.C. Error Handling & Edit WindowsSelf-Correction Window: Collectors can edit meter readings or cash inputs within a short window (10–15 minutes), provided the daily cash register hasn't been closed by the admin.Admin Override: Once locked, collectors must submit a "Correction Request" in-app, requiring admin approval.Audit Trail: All edits record original values, modified values, timestamps, and user IDs.D. Daily Cash Reconciliation (End of Day)App tracks total physical cash gathered per collector during their shift.Admins audit physical cash, compare it against recorded totals, and execute an "End of Day / Cash Lock" action to close the session and prevent retrospective tampering.5. Customer Communication (WhatsApp)Field Receipts (wa.me links): Collectors trigger instant WhatsApp receipts directly from their devices upon payment (free, direct deep-linking).Admin Broadcasts (WhatsApp API): Bulk notifications sent from the admin dashboard for monthly billing notifications, payment reminders, and disconnection warnings.6. Operational Expenses & Net Profit TrackingA. Expense TrackingVariable Expenses: Fuel/Diesel (liters, total cash, exchange rate), Oil & Filter changes, Emergency Repairs/Maintenance.Fixed Expenses: Salaries (collectors/technicians), Site Rent, Utilities, Miscellaneous.Documentation: Option to upload photos of expense receipts via mobile/desktop.B. Net Profit Calculation$$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Actual Physical Cash Collected} - \text{Total Operational Expenses}$$7. Key Reports & DashboardsA. Admin Dashboard KPIsTotal Invoiced Amount vs. Actual Cash CollectedCollection Rate Percentage (%)Cumulative Outstanding DebtTotal Active Subscribers (Metered vs. Flat-Rate)Total kWh Consumed Across NetworkB. Detailed Administrative ReportsCollector Cash Reconciliation Report: Daily cash collections per collector, pending versus audited status, and lock triggers.Overdue & Aging Debt Report: Color-coded subscriber risk tracking:Green: Fully paid / Up-to-dateYellow: 1 Month overdueRed: 2+ Months overdueDisconnection Action List: Route-grouped exportable/printable lists of non-paying subscribers for field technicians.Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement: Monthly financial breakdown showing gross revenue, expense categories, and net profit margins.Consumption Anomaly Report: Highlights zero usage, drastic consumption spikes, or drops to detect broken meters or power theft.8. Multi-Language & Arabic Localization SupportA. Dual-Language InterfaceFull system support for English and Arabic with 1-tap language switching across all web and mobile views.Right-to-Left (RTL) layout optimization for Arabic interfaces across field collector and admin panels.B. Arabic Receipts & CommunicationWhatsApp Receipts (wa.me): Auto-generated receipt text formatted in Arabic (e.g., القراءة الحالية, القراءة السابقة, الاستهلاك, المبلغ المدفوع, الباقي).Customizable Templates: Admin broadcast templates available in Arabic for bill dispatches, reminders, and disconnection warnings.C. Localized Operational TermsUI labels optimized for Lebanese generator domain context (e.g., اشتراك, أمبير, عداد, كلوواط, محصل/فتاشي, قاطع/دكتور).D. Exports & PrintablesPDF and Excel exports (Disconnection Lists, P&L Reports, Cash Audits) render natively in Arabic with proper font embedding and RTL text alignment.
